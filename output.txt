Part 1: Prolog, backward chaining

The lab's two example queries:
  ?- ancestor(john,tom).          true    john -> mary -> susan -> tom
  ?- mother(mary,susan).          true    mary is susan's parent and female

The three Task 3 queries:
  ?- brother(tom,alice).          false   alice is tom's daughter, not his sister
  ?- cousin(susan,alice).         false   susan is alice's grandmother
  ?- grandchild(tom,john).        false   john is tom's great-grandfather

The same three relations where they do hold:
  ?- brother(brian,alice).        true    both are tom's children and brian is male
  ?- cousin(tom,emma).            true    their parents susan and david are siblings
  ?- grandchild(tom,mary).        true    mary -> susan -> tom

The rules added in Task 2:
  ?- grandmother(mary,tom).       true    mary is tom's grandparent and female
  ?- grandfather(john,susan).     true    john is susan's grandparent and male
  ?- uncle(david,tom).            true    david is the brother of tom's mother susan
  ?- aunt(susan,emma).            true    susan is the sister of emma's father david
  ?- second_cousin(tom,jack).     true    their parents susan and laura are cousins
